About the Kalshi Leaderboard

This Kalshi leaderboard ranks the top 1,000 Kalshi traders by profit over the last day, week, month and all time. P&L is snapshotted every two hours, so you can see how each trader's profit changes over time, by category as well as in total.

What is the Kalshi leaderboard?

A ranking of Kalshi traders by profit. predicting.top tracks the top 1,000 Kalshi traders from their public Kalshi profiles and ranks them by daily, weekly, monthly and all-time profit.

Do I need a Kalshi account to see it?

No. Kalshi's own leaderboard lives inside Kalshi and traders have to opt in to appear on it. This page is public and needs no account.

How are daily, weekly and monthly Kalshi profits measured?

predicting.top records every tracked trader's Kalshi P&L every two hours. Daily, weekly and monthly profit is the change between those snapshots, so it fills in over the first days a trader is tracked.

Can I see a Kalshi trader's P&L history?

Yes. Each trader's profile shows their Kalshi P&L over time and their profit by category, such as politics, economics or sports.

How do I get my Kalshi account on the leaderboard?

Make your Kalshi profile public and submit your Kalshi username on the Get Listed page.
